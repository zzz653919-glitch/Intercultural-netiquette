01 · Introduction Presenter: Student 1

Introduction

Netiquette means good manners on the internet.

Intercultural netiquette means communicating politely online with people from different cultures.

It helps us avoid misunderstandings and respect cultural differences.

02 · Why it matters Presenter: Student 1

Importance of Intercultural Netiquette

People from different countries communicate online every day.

Different cultures have different communication styles.

Respectful communication builds trust and friendship.

Good netiquette makes online communication easier.

03 · Differences Presenter: Student 2

Cultural Differences in Online Communication

  • Some cultures prefer direct communication.
  • Other cultures prefer indirect and polite expressions.
  • Emojis may have different meanings for different people.
  • Humor and jokes may not be understood in the same way everywhere.

Example

Instead of:

“Your idea is wrong.”

Say:

“I have a different opinion.”

04 · The rules Presenter: Student 2

Rules of Intercultural Netiquette

  1. Use polite and respectful language.

  2. Respect different opinions and traditions.

  3. Avoid offensive jokes and stereotypes.

  4. Do not write in ALL CAPITAL LETTERS.

  5. Think before sending a message.

05 · Watch out Presenter: Student 3

Common Online Mistakes

Using rude or unclear words.

Example“Whatever. Just do it.”

Making jokes about someone's culture.

ExampleA joke that treats a tradition as “strange”.

Ignoring messages or replying too late without explanation.

ExampleThree days of silence, and no “Sorry for the delay.”

Using emojis that may be misunderstood.

ExampleA 🙂 or 👍 can feel cold or sarcastic to some readers.

Assuming everyone follows the same online rules.

Example“Why didn't you answer at once? Everyone does.”

Mistakes happen. What matters is noticing them and choosing a kinder way next time.

07 · Tips Presenter: Student 4

Tips for Better Online Communication

1

Start messages with a polite greeting.

“Hello! I hope you are well.”

2

Use simple and clear language.

Short sentences. Simple words.

3

Ask questions if something is unclear.

“Could you clarify this, please?”

4

Be patient with cultural differences.

Different time zones, different habits.

5

Respect privacy and personal boundaries.

Ask before sharing someone's photo.

08 · Conclusion Presenter: Student 4

Conclusion

Intercultural netiquette helps people communicate across cultures.

Respect and politeness prevent misunderstandings.

Everyone should be responsible for their online behavior.

“Different Cultures, One Online World.”
